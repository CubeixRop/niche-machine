# Commentary scripts

The format: something moving underneath the whole time (their gameplay), big captions in the middle, a picture popping up
whenever the voice names something. People stay for the story; the gameplay keeps their eyes busy.

## What to talk about
- **GTA itself:** news, the map, the characters, hidden details, how big the game is, what's new against GTA 5.
- **Anything else, with GTA 6 underneath:** a channel about Among Us roles can run on Minecraft parkour (Slip Stream does
  exactly that). Facts, stories, other games, food, history: pick a topic the user can make 50 of.

## The shape (25–50 seconds spoken)
1. **Hook (first line):** the claim with a name or a number. "GTA 6 needs 212 GB on your PS5." Not "Did you know…".
2. **Why it's true / the story:** 3–6 short sentences, one idea each.
3. **Payoff:** the surprising bit or the thing to do about it, last. Leave any ask to follow out of the script.

## Rules
- Spoken words, not written ones. Contractions. Sentences under 20 words.
- Every fact needs a source you can show if asked. Say "reportedly" for leaks; never invent a number.
- 4–8 pictures per Short. Put `{pic:id}` right before the word that names the thing.
- Write numbers the way the voice should say them if they're odd ("two hundred and twelve gigabytes" reads cleaner than
  "212GB" with the free voice; the captions come out as numbers anyway).
- Pictures: their own screenshots first, then official press images they can use, then free-licence (Commons) pictures.
  Credits land in the project's `credits.txt`: put them in the description.
