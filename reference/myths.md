# Myths and what-if tests to try in GTA 6

GrayStillPlays' three most popular Shorts are all called "Busting 5 Myths in GTA 5": three uploads with the same title,
over 160 million views between them, on a game that was already years old. Every one of those ideas can be tested again
in a brand-new game. The comments then hand you the next tests.

These are GTA 5 myths and what-ifs people argued about. Treat each as a question to film, never as a fact: the verdict is
whatever the clip shows in GTA 6.

## Survival
- Can you survive a fall if you land in a swimming pool?
- Does a parachute opened at the last second still save you?
- Can you survive jumping off the tallest building onto a car roof?
- What happens if you skydive into a moving boat?
- Can you outrun an explosion on foot?
- Does a helmet save you when you fly off a motorbike?

## Vehicles
- Can a blimp or plane fit under a highway bridge?
- Do jerry cans (gas cans) explode near fire?
- Does a car blow up if you shoot the gas tank?
- Can a car drive on the bottom of the sea?
- Can you stop a train by parking a truck on the tracks?
- Can a car with no tires still drive?

## Police and people
- Do cops ignore you if you change clothes?
- Can you lose the police by hiding under a bridge?
- Will police chase you into the water?
- Do pedestrians remember you if you come back?
- Will someone call the cops if you only wave a weapon?

## The world
- Is there fall damage in water from any height?
- Can animals get a wanted level for you?
- Do shops close at night?
- Does the weather change what NPCs do?
- Can you swim to the edge of the map?

## How to film one
1. Set it up so the viewer understands the question in the first second (show the pool, the bridge, the train).
2. One clean try. If it takes more, keep only the one that answers the question.
3. Hold on the result for a beat: that's where the BUSTED or CONFIRMED stamp lands.
