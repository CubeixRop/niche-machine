# Comparisons to film first

Game Forensics (over half a billion views) built a channel on one idea: the same action in two games, one on top of the
other, so you see how differently each game handles it. It works with the sound off. One of their Shorts compared a GTA 6
trailer with Sleeping Dogs and passed 7 million views, before GTA 6 was even out.

The method: take the GTA 4 vs GTA 5 comparisons that already did well and film the same thing in GTA 6 vs GTA 5.

## Ideas (GTA 6 vs GTA 5, or vs GTA 4 / Vice City 2002)
- **Driving:** the same car into the same wall; how cars handle a sharp turn; crash damage up close.
- **Police:** what happens at one star; how fast they arrive; how they react if you surrender; losing a wanted level.
- **Ragdoll and falls:** jump off a roof; get hit by a car; fall off a bike at speed (GTA 4's physics are the classic
  benchmark).
- **Water:** jump into the sea from a pier; drive a car into water; swim underwater; boats in waves.
- **NPCs:** bump into someone; pull a weapon in a crowd; honk at a pedestrian; what people do in the rain.
- **Explosions and fire:** a car fire; a gas station; a grenade on a parked car.
- **Weather and light:** rain on the street; a sunset; a storm.
- **The map:** the new Vice City next to the old one (Ocean Drive, the beach, the Malibu Club spot), the same street in
  daylight in both.
- **Small details:** shooting a tire; breaking a window; buying food; the phone; getting a haircut.
- **Wanted level max:** what the highest wanted level brings in each game (GTA 4 had six stars; GTA 5 dropped to five).

## Rules
- Line the two up on the same beat (the impact, the splash). 5–8 seconds a pair, 2–5 pairs a Short.
- Same camera angle and time of day in both when you can. Label both games and the years.
- Their own footage in both halves, or official trailer footage clearly labelled. Never another channel's comparison.
