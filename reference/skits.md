# Skits: a story with no words

GTA Gaming (over 445 million views) makes up short stories with Franklin, Michael and Trevor. Many have the same simple
setup: a character has to rescue someone. Franklin saving a shark stuck on the shore passed 134 million views. You
understand the whole story just by looking at it.

## The shape (20–50 seconds, 6–12 shots)
1. **The problem (1–2 shots):** someone or something in trouble, obvious at a glance (stranded, chased, stuck, lost).
2. **The hero notices (1 shot):** a character the audience knows.
3. **The try (2–5 shots):** the hero goes for it; one thing goes wrong.
4. **The save (1–2 shots):** it works, often in an unexpected way.
5. **The button (1 shot):** a small joke or a reaction at the end.

## A shot line
| # | What happens | Who | Where on the map | Time / weather | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A shark flaps on the sand | (animal) | the beach | noon, sunny | low, close |

## Rules
- No dialogue needed: if a line is needed, put it on screen as one short speech bubble (`on_screen`), at most three in
  the whole Short.
- Characters people already know, in new situations.
- Film more than you need: each shot 5–10 seconds, so the edit can trim to the action.
- Director mode / the Rockstar Editor made this easy in GTA 5 (pick the character, time of day, weather, stage the
  scene). If GTA 6 has them, use them; if not, film while playing and stage it with friends online.
