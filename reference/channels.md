# The channels, grouped by niche

Every channel from the video, with what to study on it. Numbers are what their pages showed in mid-November 2026
(they only go up). Open each one's Shorts tab and sort by **Popular**: that's the list of what already worked.

## 1. Commentary over gameplay
- **Drifty**: the Short "GTA 6's file size is MASSIVE", over 5 million views. GTA gameplay underneath, a picture pops up
  for everything he names. Search the title on YouTube.
- **Slip Stream** (https://www.youtube.com/@RealSlip/shorts): over 186 million views, a whole channel about Among Us
  (roles, glitches, theories) with Minecraft parkour underneath. Proof the topic and the gameplay don't have to match.

## 2. Game comparisons
- **Game Forensics** (https://www.youtube.com/@gameforensics/shorts): over half a billion views. GTA 4 vs GTA 5, GTA 5 vs
  Hitman, Red Dead vs Red Dead. "GTA 6 vs Sleeping Dogs" passed 7 million views with GTA 6 footage from a trailer.

## 3. Stunts
- **Leonida Stunts** (https://www.youtube.com/@leonidastunts/shorts): over 960 million views. "The ending caught me off
  guard" (a plane through two train tunnels, "attempt #69") passed 73 million. The fails first, the landing last.
- **Onegamesplus** (https://www.youtube.com/@onegamesplus/shorts): the bigger version, superhero characters and custom
  ramps.

## 4. Discoveries and myths
- **GrayStillPlays** (https://www.youtube.com/@GrayStillPlays/shorts): the three most popular Shorts are all "Busting 5
  Myths in GTA 5", over 160 million views between them. Same title three times.

## 5. Moments
- **BullyZone** (https://www.youtube.com/@bullyzone/shorts): over 620 million views, scenes from different GTA games.

## 6. Rankings
- **Define Ranked** (https://www.youtube.com/@DefineRanked/shorts): over 142 million views from 42 uploads, ranking
  everything from dogs to drive-through moments.
- **Leonida Stunts** again: "Ranking My Most Legendary GTA V moments" and "Top 5 Davey moments in GTA 5", over 100 million
  views between the two.

## Bonus: skits
- **GTA Gaming** (https://www.youtube.com/@gtagaming007/shorts): over 445 million views, stories with Franklin, Michael and
  Trevor. "Shark stuck at the Shore! Can Franklin reach in time?" passed 134 million. They say they use the Rockstar
  Editor.

## How to study a channel in ten minutes
1. Shorts tab → Popular. Write down the top 10 titles.
2. What's the same in the top 5? (the title shape, the first second, the length, the ending)
3. Which of those can you make with GTA 6 this week? That's your first five Shorts.
